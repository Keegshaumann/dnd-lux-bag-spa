The Spa Treatment

Every Bag Deserves
Expert Hands.

Deep Cleaning

Full interior lining extraction and cleaning. Exterior canvas, leather, and suede treatment. Stain extraction using specialist solvents that preserve material integrity.

Leather Conditioning & Restoration

Premium leather moisturiser restores suppleness and prevents cracking. Colour matching and pigment touch-up. Crack and scuff treatment that blends seamlessly with original material.

Hardware Polishing

Brass, gold-tone, and silver hardware restored to factory lustre. Zip pulls, clasps, buckles, and studs treated individually. Tarnish removal without damage to plating or patina.
